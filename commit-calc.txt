Run the plate model in the case study as a working calculator

The calculator tab was a disabled chip; it now prices a plate in the browser
against the project's own exported weights. Three letter selects in printed
order, digits, market, and plate type behind an advanced disclosure. The result
carries the ranges, the comparable group's size, the typical error factor, the
model version and the date it was trained through, so the answer arrives with
its own evidence rather than as a bare number.

Weights load lazily: 138 kB raw, 24.7 kB over the wire, only when the tab opens.

The evidence block on the case study also moves from the README's example to
the export's measured figures for fair-price-v4 trained through 2026-11-03:
2,001 plates held out, typical error ×1.35, wide-band coverage 91-93 per cent,
rank correlation 0.78 against a segment-median baseline of 0.63.
